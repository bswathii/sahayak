Meaning, not media.

Sahayak connects a student and a volunteer over text and vector drawings only — no audio, no video. A local Kannada glossary handles common phrases without any cloud request, and AI translation is a fallback that can fail without interrupting the lesson.

Stick 'Em
Tallest Tower Challenge

A student builds the tallest free-standing tower they can from chopsticks and connectors, and works through ten stages: choose, understand, question, predict, plan, build, test, explain, improve, reflect.

Material counts, the official measuring rule and any scoring are pending human confirmation — they are not shown as facts. Reference: academy.stickem.sg/guest

Open the student view → Join with a session code. Work through the ten stages, ask your own question, sketch a plan, receive messages and drawings, and record what happened. Open the volunteer view → Create or join a session, follow the student's stage, draw explanations, send messages and read the student's own answers.
